Privacy Policy

Last Updated:

This Privacy Policy describes how CXShield ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). By using our App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Camera Permission

Camera Access: Our App requires access to your device's camera to provide core functionality. We request camera permission only when necessary for the App's features to work properly.

When you grant camera permission, the App may access your device's camera to:

  • Capture photos or videos as part of the App's core functionality
  • Process images in real-time for security and privacy features
  • Enable camera-based features that you choose to use

Important: We do not store, transmit, or share any images, photos, or videos captured through the camera without your explicit consent. All camera data is processed locally on your device.

2. How We Use Camera Data

Camera access is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To provide the core functionality of the App
  • To process images locally on your device
  • To enable features that require camera access

We do not:

  • Store camera images or videos on our servers
  • Share camera data with third parties
  • Use camera data for advertising or marketing purposes
  • Access your camera when the App is not in active use

3. Data Storage and Security

All camera processing occurs locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit any camera data to external servers. Any images or videos you choose to save are stored only on your device, and you have full control over this data.

4. Third-Party Services

Our App does not share camera data with third-party services. We may use third-party services for analytics or crash reporting, but these services do not have access to your camera or any images captured through the App.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding camera access:

  • You can grant or revoke camera permission at any time through your device settings
  • You control when the camera is accessed (only when you actively use camera features)
  • You can uninstall the App at any time, which will revoke all permissions

To revoke camera permission:

  • Android: Settings → Apps → CXShield → Permissions → Camera → Deny

6. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
